1. Executive summary

  • Across 134 non-overlapping out-of-sample origins and a 5-bar horizon, the lowest MAE (price units, all steps) came from arima (1.1754).
  • drift: MAE 1.017x the naive random walk (Diebold-Mariano p=0.147 at bar 5); directional accuracy 48.5% (95% CI 40.2%-56.9%, n=134), not statistically different from 50%.
  • arima: MAE 0.998x the naive random walk (Diebold-Mariano p=0.586 at bar 5); directional accuracy 54.5% (95% CI 46.0%-62.7%, n=134), not statistically different from 50%.
  • kronos_mini: MAE 1.196x the naive random walk (Diebold-Mariano p=0.002 at bar 5); directional accuracy 49.3% (95% CI 40.9%-57.6%, n=134), not statistically different from 50%.
  • Forecast-accuracy results (this section) are reported separately from the research backtest (section 6); a model can be more accurate without producing a better simulated outcome, and vice versa.

3. Protocol

  • Expanding-window walk-forward: 134 origins from 2024-05-09 to 2026-03-05, step 5 bars (evaluation windows do not overlap), embargo 0 bar(s) on parameter estimation, minimum history 128 bars.
  • At each origin every model receives only bars up to and including the origin; forecasts cover the next 5 bars of close. Future timestamps given to Kronos are generated from the bar calendar, not read from the file.
  • Models:
    • naive: Random walk: every future close equals the last observed close.
    • drift: Random walk with drift: mean log-return over the lookback window, extrapolated.
    • arima: ARIMA(1, 1, 1) on log close, refit every 1 origin(s)
    • kronos_mini: Kronos-mini zero-shot (4,108,032 params), context 256, mean of 5 samples, T=1.0, top_p=0.9, CPU — weights NeoQuasar/Kronos-mini@f4e6869, tokenizer NeoQuasar/Kronos-Tokenizer-2k@26966d0
  • Metrics: MAE / RMSE / MAPE over all 5 steps; relative MAE vs naive; Diebold-Mariano test on squared errors at bar 5 vs naive; directional accuracy of the bar-5 move with Wilson 95% CI and a two-sided binomial test vs 50% (origins where the model or the market shows no change are excluded).

8. Limitations

  • One instrument, one horizon, one historical period. Results may not transfer to other instruments, regimes or horizons.
  • Kronos is evaluated zero-shot with fixed sampling settings; other settings, larger variants or fine-tuning may behave differently. Its training data is not public, so contamination cannot be ruled out for periods before its release.
  • ARIMA uses a fixed order on log prices; no order search was performed.
  • Statistical tests are not adjusted for multiple comparisons across models.
  • The research backtest ignores slippage beyond the bps assumption, funding, borrow, market impact and capacity.
